fcaz
====

Property and casualty actuarial modeling in Python, built to feel familiar to users of
:mod:`scipy.stats` and scikit-learn. Distribution objects are used the way scipy's are, with
actuarial extras such as the hazard function and finite mixtures, and with parameterizations
that follow *Loss Models* (Klugman, Panjer, Willmot).
